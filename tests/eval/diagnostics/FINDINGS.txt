Strongest established finding: implementation mismatch
Upstream ObservationPack replaces large historical observations in the messages
sent to the provider; our evidence archive does not change provider history.
Upstream Online Context Compact invokes native context.compact; our continuation
notes do not remove history. Upstream Action Fusion extends edit/write schemas
with then_run; our skill registers no such tools. The upstream reducer intercepts
tool results, invokes a configured reducer, and returns a checked receipt; our
helper is manual and has no tool-result interception.
Sources: SoL-Pi/src/sol-pi/extensions/{observation-pack,action-fusion,
online-context-compact,evidence-preserving-reducer}/ and
skills/efficient-coding/references/execution.md.
These interface facts are directly verified. They establish that this benchmark
evaluates a prompt adaptation, not the original runtime mechanisms. They do not
prove that adding those mechanisms to this setup will reproduce upstream savings.

Outliers and uncertainty
The largest sol-pi cost increase is django__django-11149/r2: +2,342,865 tokens,
60 requests versus baseline's 42, and 24 tool validation errors versus 1.
Both resolve. This cell is 79% of the final net token increase. Removing the
whole task leaves a positive difference of +40,139 tokens/run, versus +123,642
with all tasks. Thus the average is strongly influenced by outliers, but the
positive point estimate is not solely the result of that single task.
14/24 sol-pi cells are more expensive and 10/24 cheaper. Combined is more
expensive in 11/24 cells and cheaper in 13/24; its mean does not describe a
consistent per-run regression.

The refreshed analyzer resamples whole tasks while retaining all rounds and
paired arms, with 5,000 bootstrap samples and seed 42. SWE-bench 95% intervals:
- Sol-pi minus baseline, tokens/run: [-139,595, +393,584].
- Combined minus karpathy, tokens/run: [-184,228, +327,651].
- Sol-pi minus baseline, tokens/verified solve: [-199,461, +470,453].
- Combined minus karpathy, tokens/verified solve: [-305,177, +370,937].
No SWE-bench savings or regression is statistically established by these intervals.
Per-task resolution differences average to zero even for the task whose rounds
trade wins/losses, so this clustered empirical resolution interval degenerates
to [0,0]. That is a sampling limitation, not evidence of exact equivalence.
Stress sol-pi-minus-baseline has a positive task-cluster interval [1,102, 27,563]
tokens/run, whereas the old cell-wise interval crosses zero. Only five clusters
are available, so do not overstate this sensitivity result. Both methods and
limitations are retained in skill-diagnosis.json.

Supported correction versus hypotheses
The skill's main entrypoint contained irrelevant delegation and measurement
instructions for ordinary single-agent tasks. Removing them reduces injected
instruction size; it is a defensible low-risk correction. The mechanism mismatch
is documented, rather than hidden behind claims of prompt-only compaction/fusion.
Whether shorter instructions reduce retries or total task tokens remains a
hypothesis requiring new observations. A prompt cannot repair missing runtime
hooks, and this revision does not claim to do so.

It does not meet the project's baseline-saving goal in this smoke check.
All 15 usage/request totals and source hashes were verified. Result source:
eval/revision_eval/summary.json, manifest.json, and trace-review.json.

Counterexample: the revised fabricated-quote case uses 387,407 tokens versus
249,422 original and 197,318 baseline. Its first request reads the full log
before successfully loading the skill, so input jumps from 15,679 to 42,193
on the next request. Skill loading succeeds in request 5 of 9; 190,054 input
tokens have already been spent through that call, out of 381,297 input tokens.
The 48,054-byte serialized result then remains in subsequent request history.
Thus even a shorter skill cannot retroactively eliminate that replay. The
malformed calls also begin before the skill body is loaded, countering a simple
story that the body itself causes every tool failure. Metadata effects and
run variance remain possible; the trace is not a causal ablation.

Assessment after correction
The shipped instruction-level revision removes demonstrable loading bulk and
unnecessary administrative requirements, and passes the behavioral smoke cases.
It remains an unproven efficiency candidate. Reproducing upstream runtime gains
requires an independently evaluated harness integration; no such integration
was installed in this task. Do not describe the revision as a validated
baseline-beating optimization or use this five-case pilot to claim noninferiority.
